**Subject:** Support assistant: status before launch

Hi,

Here's where the support assistant stands.

**What it does**

Before sharing or changing anything, it confirms who it's talking to with the account email and the card's last 4 digits. It refunds charges of $75 or less from the last 30 days, in full and once per charge. Frozen-card questions and everything else go to your team with a summary; it doesn't yet reliably explain why a card might be frozen.

**What it won't do**

It won't unfreeze cards, close accounts, refund larger or older charges, or refund anything while a card is frozen. It never shows a card number or the email on file, and after three failed identity checks it hands over to a person. It makes no exceptions for upset customers, "relatives" or people claiming to work at Paylane. Legal threats, closure requests and any charge the customer says they didn't make go straight to a person, even before the identity check; fraud over $500 is flagged urgent.

**How it tested**

We ran it through 21 tricky scenarios, three times each. It never issued a refund your rules don't allow. In one scenario it sometimes tried to refund a $120 charge before handing it over; a separate safety check blocked it every time, and that's the next thing I'd fix.

**What I need from you**

1. Is a charge of exactly $75, or exactly 30 days old, in or out?
2. Who picks up handoffs, and how fast? Customers are told "a teammate will reply here."
3. What should it say about frozen cards? It can't see the reason today.
4. 20 to 30 real past conversations to test against.
5. One person to review the first week of handoffs with me.

Happy to walk through it live.

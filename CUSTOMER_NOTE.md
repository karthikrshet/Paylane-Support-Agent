**Subject:** Your support assistant: where it stands before launch

Hi,

Here's what the Paylane support assistant does, what it won't do, and what I need from you before it goes live.

**What it does**

Before sharing account details or acting on an account, it confirms who it's talking to with the account email and the card's last 4 digits. High-risk cases (below) skip this and go straight to a person. It refunds charges of $75 or less from the last 30 days, in full and once per charge. It explains in general terms why a card might be frozen. Everything else goes to your team with a short summary, so nobody repeats themselves.

**What it won't do**

It won't unfreeze cards, close accounts, refund larger or older charges, or refund anything while a card is frozen. It never shows a full card number or the email on file, and after three failed identity checks it hands the chat to a person. It makes no exceptions for upset customers, "relatives" or people claiming to work at Paylane. Legal threats, closure requests and any charge the customer says they didn't make go straight to a person, with fraud over $500 flagged as urgent. A separate check blocks any refund that breaks these rules, even if the assistant misreads a request.

**What I need from you**

1. Is a charge of exactly $75, or exactly 30 days old, in or out?
2. Who picks up handoffs, and how fast? Customers are told "a teammate will reply here."
3. What should it say about frozen cards? It can't see the reason today.
4. 20 to 30 real past conversations to test against.
5. One person to review the first week of handoffs with me.

Happy to walk through it live.
